      How to play

      Your ship starts facing the way its bow points. Add orders, then press Set sail. Pressing Ahead again right after an Ahead order makes that order longer, up to 4 squares. Each order counts as one, however far it goes.

      Keyboard: ↑ ahead, ← → turn 45°, Q / E turn 90°, Enter to sail, Backspace to undo.

      Reach the ✕ without hitting a rock or sailing off the chart. If you match par, you get ★★★.
